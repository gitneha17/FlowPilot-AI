# 2–3 Minute Demo Video Plan

Record the app running on your own computer. Do not narrate features you have not tested.

## Suggested timeline (about 2 minutes 30 seconds)
- **0:00–0:20 — Problem:** "Small teams can spend time sorting inquiries, prioritizing urgent issues, and drafting repetitive replies."
- **0:20–0:40 — Solution:** Show FlowPilot AI dashboard and explain the workflow.
- **0:40–1:05 — Add an inquiry:** Enter a fictional example mentioning an urgent website issue. Show the assigned category, priority, and next action.
- **1:05–1:30 — CSV import:** Import `sample_inquiries.csv`. Explain how empty and duplicate records are handled; show the result you observed.
- **1:30–1:50 — Search, filter, status:** Demonstrate these only after confirming they work.
- **1:50–2:10 — Draft a reply:** Click Draft reply. Explain whether the result came from the local template or optional Gemini API, and remind viewers that a human must review it.
- **2:10–2:25 — Export:** Download the CSV and open it briefly.
- **2:25–2:40 — Limitations and next step:** Explain that the prototype uses in-memory storage and rule-based classification; mention SQLite and evaluation as future improvements.

## Recording checklist
- Close private tabs and notifications.
- Use only fictional sample data.
- Make browser text readable.
- Record the app and your voice using a free screen recorder available on your computer.
- Keep the video unlisted if using a video hosting service, or share it via a cloud drive with viewer access.
- Verify the link works in a private/incognito window.
- Never claim performance improvements unless you measured them.
